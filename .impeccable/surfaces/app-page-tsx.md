Scope: the whole site, twenty-six routes. Visitor mode is Persuade on the
landing, join, register and policy routes; Operate on garage, search, vehicle,
experiences, shops, settings, review and admin.

Audience: a US owner holding a quote they cannot check, and the enthusiast who
knows the car by chassis code. Action: file a price. Proof: shops are real,
prices are not yet, so every example figure is labelled and empty states are
primary surfaces. Constraints: strict CSP, self-hosted fonts only, contrast
suite, 44px targets, reduced-motion, frozen routes and IA.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Gaari is the copy of the repair order the owner keeps, where every line
is an operation reconciling to a total. It refuses the category's car photograph
under a centred search field.

OWN-WORLD: Near-white form stock, blue-black impact ink, process blue for
structure, one stamp red reserved for reconciled and receipt-verified. Hairline
column rules and ruled regions, never cards. Tabular figures everywhere; grotesk
for prose, mono for codes and money. Every grey is the ink at opacity. Material
translated from the artifact's canary and pink carbon copies to the top copy
alone, because novelty stock collides with the brief's minimal register; the
topology, controls, state vocabulary and ritual are kept whole.

STORY: The visitor reads one real itemised record, understands that a price here
is reconciled and receipt-backed rather than quoted, finds their own generation
in the index, and files theirs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One record at document scale, full width. Header block carries
the generation and chassis code. Ruled operation lines fill the left, PARTS and
LABOR columns right-aligned beside them. Reconciliation total bottom right, range
scale with a drawn leader line bottom left, stamp landing on reconcile. The
primary action sits at the signature line, bottom right.

FORM: The Repair Order; candidate 3 of 7 on the grounded list; seed 102e37bb.

Raises: total ink commitment (Risograph); annotation as navigation (Dress Code);
type mass carries value and restacks rather than shrinks (BLAST); the
verification tag stays attached (Industrial Quote Grammar); one reserved accent
marks only what is live (Automata).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance
